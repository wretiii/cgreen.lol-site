This session reviewed emerging technology risks and regulatory requirements, then translated them into practical steps for building a complete security program. The presentation covered AI governance, post-quantum readiness, executive accountability, CMMC, HIPAA, segmentation, PCI DSS, privacy, risk management, zero trust, and penetration testing.

## CMMC Phase II Update

On July 13, 2026, the Department of War announced the immediate suspension of CMMC Phase II requirements and a 60-day program review. Phase I self-assessment requirements remained in effect, along with enforcement of NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 through self-assessments and selected government-led assessments.
