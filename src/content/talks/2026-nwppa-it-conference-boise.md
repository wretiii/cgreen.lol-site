This session introduced the NERC CIP regulatory ecosystem and a practical path to compliance for small utilities, cooperatives, and municipal providers. It covered registered-entity obligations, BES Cyber Asset identification, high/medium/low impact classification, LERC and LEAP, low-impact controls, evidence collection, common audit findings, and a focused 90-day action plan.

## 90-Day Starting Priorities

1. Confirm registration, applicable functions, and the designated CIP Senior Manager.
2. Inventory and classify potential BES Cyber Assets, including a physical walk-through of substations and control centers.
3. Document the cybersecurity policy, low-impact plan, physical and electronic access controls, transient-device controls, and incident-response process.
4. Collect dated, versioned, and approved evidence that demonstrates each control is operating.
5. Subscribe to NERC, WECC, and E-ISAC updates and engage peer utilities through NWPPA.
